# Storyboard & Production Directions

**Target Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 Vertical Video (1080 x 1920)  
**Total Running Time**: 55 seconds  
**Voice Tone**: Engaging, fast-paced, authoritative tech YouTube narrator (similar to Fireship / Linus Tech Tips Shorts).

---

## Scene 1: The Hook (00:00 - 00:06)
- **Framing**: Split-screen vertical cut (top half / bottom half or side-by-side zoom).
- **Top Shot**: High-end modern desktop rig with RGB lighting and liquid tubes.
- **Bottom Shot**: Vintage Dell OptiPlex 790 or ThinkPad T420 running on a wooden desk.
- **Graphic Overlay**: Red question mark badge: `15-YR OLD PC > $10K RIG?`
- **Audio Cue**: Bass drop / record scratch when switching to the vintage PC.

## Scene 2: The Energy Waste Reality (00:06 - 00:18)
- **Framing**: Full vertical slide with dynamic charts.
- **Visual**: Quick clip of industrial ASIC mining containers and a rising power meter.
- **Graphic Overlay**: `Traditional PoW: Millions of Joules wasted`.
- **Transitions**: Fast horizontal whoosh transition.

## Scene 3: The Terminal Reveal (00:18 - 00:32)
- **Framing**: 9:16 terminal screen recording using dark theme (monokai / catppuccin) with large font (24pt).
- **Terminal Execution**:
  ```bash
  $ ./rustchain_miner --telemetry
  [+] Detected CPU: Intel(R) Core(TM) i5-2400 CPU @ 3.10GHz
  [+] Microarchitecture: Sandy Bridge (Release: 2011)
  [+] Antiquity Age: 15 Years
  [+] Proof-of-Antiquity Multiplier: 2.708x BONUS
  [+] Mining epoch 1042... Nonce FOUND! Block #58291 ACCEPTED!
  ```
- **Graphic Overlay**: Highlight box in neon green around `2.708x BONUS`.

## Scene 4: The Antiquity Formula (00:32 - 00:46)
- **Framing**: Motion graphic card with clean typography.
- **Visual**: 
  - Formula: $\text{Target} = T_0 \times \alpha(\text{age}) \times \beta(\text{cores})$
  - Bar chart showing Sandy Bridge earning high normalized reward per watt, while Threadripper is throttled by the core penalty factor.
- **Graphic Overlay**: `Proof-of-Antiquity: True Hardware Decentralization`.

## Scene 5: Outro & Call to Action (00:46 - 00:55)
- **Framing**: Clean browser mockup displaying `https://github.com/Scottcjn/Rustchain`.
- **Text on Screen**: 
  - `RustChain: Reviving Vintage Hardware`
  - `Star & Join: github.com/Scottcjn/Rustchain`
  - Author credit: `Package by @tivince82`
- **Audio Cue**: Upbeat synth swell and chime.
